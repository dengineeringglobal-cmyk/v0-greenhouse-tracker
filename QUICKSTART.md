# Farm Tracker - Quick Start Guide

## What is Farm Tracker?

Farm Tracker is a comprehensive farm management system that helps you:
- Monitor and manage multiple farms
- Track crop lifecycle from planting to harvest
- Record farm activities and operations
- Monitor harvest yields and quality
- Track expenses and budget management
- Generate detailed reports and analytics
- Access agricultural tips and best practices

## Getting Started in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Login (30 seconds)
1. Open the application
2. Click "Login as Demo User" button
3. You're now logged in with admin privileges

### Step 2: Create Your First Farm (1 minute)
1. Click "Farms" in the left sidebar
2. Click "Add Farm" button
3. Enter farm name, location, and total area
4. Click "Create Farm"

### Step 3: Add Your First Crop (1 minute)
1. Click "Crops" in the sidebar
2. Click "Add Crop" button
3. Fill in crop details:
   - Crop Name (e.g., "Bell Pepper")
   - Variety (e.g., "Red Bell Pepper F1")
   - Planting Date
   - Estimated Harvest Date
   - Area in square meters
   - Status (choose "Growing" for active crops)
4. Click "Create Crop"

### Step 4: Log Your First Activity (1 minute)
1. Click "Activities" in the sidebar
2. Click "Log Activity" button
3. Select your crop
4. Choose activity type (Fertilizing, Irrigation, etc.)
5. Add description and date
6. Click "Log Activity"

### Step 5: View Your Dashboard (1 minute)
1. Click "Dashboard" in the sidebar
2. See your farm metrics and charts
3. View recent activities you just logged

## Common Tasks

### To Add a New Farm:
Farms → Add Farm → Fill details → Create

### To Track a Harvest:
Harvest → Record Harvest → Select crop → Enter details → Record

### To Log Expenses:
Expenses → Record Expense → Select category → Enter amount → Record

### To View Analytics:
Dashboard → View charts or Reports → Generate report

### To Export Data:
Reports → Export Excel (downloads spreadsheet)

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Daily Check-in**: Review dashboard every morning
2. **Log Activities Promptly**: Record operations same day
3. **Accurate Dates**: Use correct dates for accurate trends
4. **Detailed Notes**: Add descriptions for context
5. **Regular Backups**: Export Excel monthly
6. **Review Reports**: Check monthly reports for insights
